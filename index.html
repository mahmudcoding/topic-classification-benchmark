Practical limits compared with an LLM

  • Limited room for text and labels. With our settings, GLiClass considered about 55 labels per message and GLiNER 240, out of 860. A shortlist can leave out the right label.
  • More labels mean more work. BGE and mDeBERTa compare each message with each label separately. Thousands of labels require thousands of comparisons.
  • Missing context needs a separate decision. These runs can return no labels, but do not distinguish “no matching topic” from “not enough context.” That needs an explicit label or another check.
  • Limited answers. These runs return supplied labels and scores. They do not write explanations or suggest new topics.
  • Fine details get mixed up. Ultra added “repair work” to an outage notice that gave no reason. A high score can still be wrong.

A long-context LLM has room for more labels, rules and conversation history together. It can also be asked to explain choices, flag missing information or suggest new topics. It can still make mistakes.

How we tested: 1,000 real Telegram posts, comments and group messages from a frozen set of 32,245. We used 200 to choose settings, then scored the other 800. GLiClass and GLiNER received the closest labels that fit their input; BGE and mDeBERTa checked all 860. Missed shortlist labels count as misses.
